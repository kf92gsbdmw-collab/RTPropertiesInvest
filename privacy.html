Privacy notice

This notice explains how RT Invest Properties (“we”) handles personal data on this website, in line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Draft: to be checked by RT's legal adviser before publication.

Controller

RT Invest Properties, address, TODO@rtproperties.ch.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form and e-mail: name, e-mail address, phone number if given, the property you are interested in and your message. We use this only to answer you and to arrange viewings. Legal basis: performance of pre-contractual steps at your request.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider (GitHub Pages / Netlify / Vercel) records technical data such as IP address, browser type, time of access and the page requested, for security and operation of the site.
  • Analytics: none / Plausible (no cookies, no personal data) / Google Analytics 4 with consent banner.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies of its own. Adjust if analytics or embedded maps are added.

Sharing and transfers

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data may be processed by our hosting and e-mail providers, some of which may be located outside Switzerland and the EU; in that case we rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

Retention

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless a contract follows or the law requires longer retention.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to processing, and request a copy in a portable format by writing to TODO@rtproperties.ch. You may also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or your local supervisory authority.

External links

Links to Instagram and other third-party sites are subject to those providers' own privacy policies.
