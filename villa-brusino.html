From usDraft

The house faces south-west, so the terrace keeps the sun until the mountains across the lake take it, around nine in July. We kept the split levels when we renovated; they are what make a 390 m² house feel like a sequence of rooms rather than one big hall. The dock below takes three boats, and Porto Ceresio is five minutes by car if you would rather go by road. The shop, the post office and the tennis club are a short walk.

Location

Brusino Arsizio sits on the quiet southern shore of Lake Lugano, below Monte San Giorgio. Lugano is about twenty-five minutes by car or a short boat ride across; Porto Ceresio and the Italian border are five minutes away.
